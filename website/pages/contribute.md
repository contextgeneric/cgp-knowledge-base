# Contribute

The Contribute page lists the ways to help CGP, from the smallest commitment to the largest, and
closes with the author's sponsorship section and the project's acknowledgement.

- **URL:** <https://contextgeneric.dev/docs/contribute>
- **Source:** [docs/contribute.md](https://github.com/contextgeneric/contextgeneric.dev/blob/main/docs/contribute.md)
- **Status:** Current

## How the page was made

An agent rewrote the page to [the orientation guide](../writing-guides/orientation.md#the-contribute-page),
which the author allowed in full, and its foot carries the provenance line for documentation pages.
The sponsorship section and the acknowledgement are the author's text, unchanged apart from the
acknowledgement's heading level and its Hermes SDK link, which the author removed.

## What it covers

The opening names Context-Generic Programming beside a plain description and links the
Introduction, without the tag line, then says the routes run from the smallest commitment to the
largest and that each helps on its own. Five routes follow in project voice:

- **Join the discussion:** GitHub Discussions, the Discord server, and the r/cgp subreddit.
- **Report a problem:** a confusing or wrong page goes to GitHub Discussions or the website
  repository's issue tracker; a problem in the knowledge base goes to the base's issue tracker, with
  the reporting section of *Contributing to CGP with an Agent* for what to include.
- **Write about CGP and share it:** independent posts, tutorials, and talks, and the Bluesky
  account.
- **Publish reusable components:** the aim of an ecosystem where most users write only the wiring
  that selects published components and providers, stated as an aim that does not exist yet.
- **Change CGP itself:** one paragraph routing to *Contributing to CGP with an Agent*, the site's
  only guide to changing CGP.

*Sponsor Me* is the author's first-person account, and the acknowledgement credits Soares Chen as
CGP's creator, names Haskell typeclasses as the primary inspiration, and records that CGP was
developed at [Informal Systems](https://informal.systems/) as part of the Hermes SDK, which it names
without a link, since the SDK is a
[legacy resource](https://github.com/contextgeneric/contextgeneric.dev/blob/main/AGENTS.md#legacy-resources).

## How it relates to the knowledge base

The page is the top rung of the conversion ladder in
[formats.md](../../communication-strategy/formats.md#the-conversion-ladder), the ask made of the
enthusiast profile in [readers.md](../../communication-strategy/readers.md). Its components section
restates the aim the [hypershell post](../blog/hypershell-release.md) argues at length, that
developers who write reusable components serve those who only wire them, and
[message.md](../../communication-strategy/message.md#when-not-to-reach-for-cgp) could draw on it
more. The reporting route follows
[contributions from outside the project](../../communication-strategy/ai-disclosure.md#contributions-from-outside-the-project).

## Maintaining it

- The sponsorship section is in the author's first-person voice and candid about finances. Do not
  rewrite it into project prose; it is the one place on the site where a single voice belongs.
- The acknowledgement names the Hermes SDK without linking it; do not add the link back.
- The page may link the knowledge base's issue tracker and nothing else in the base, per the
  website's [link rules](https://github.com/contextgeneric/contextgeneric.dev/blob/main/AGENTS.md#links).
- The heading and the sidebar label are both *Contribute*, so the full name opens the prose.
