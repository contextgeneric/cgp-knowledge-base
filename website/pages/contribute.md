# Contribute

The Contribute page lists the ways to help CGP and closes with the project's acknowledgement.

- **URL:** <https://contextgeneric.dev/docs/contribute>
- **Source:** [docs/contribute.md](https://github.com/contextgeneric/contextgeneric.dev/blob/main/docs/contribute.md)
- **Status:** Current

## What it covers

The page lists seven contribution paths: joining the discussion channels, reading and critiquing the
documentation, publishing reusable CGP components, contributing code or documentation, sharing on
social media, writing independently about CGP, and sponsoring the author. The code-or-documentation
path routes to the [contributing page](knowledge-base.md), the site's only guide to changing CGP
itself, and to its section on reporting problems in the base.

The "publish your own components" section frames the project's goal as an ecosystem where most users
only write wiring, so modularity benefits people who do not care about it.

An acknowledgement follows, crediting Soares Chen as CGP's creator, naming Haskell typeclasses as the
primary inspiration, and recording that CGP was developed at
[Informal Systems](https://informal.systems/) as part of the
[Hermes SDK](https://github.com/informalsystems/hermes-sdk/).

## Where it diverges from the guide

The page predates [the orientation guide](../writing-guides/orientation.md#the-contribute-page), and
the author allowed it to be rewritten in full. Its routes are the right ones, but it diverges in five
ways:

- The opening and the documentation section speak as a corporate "we" and "us".
- The opening never names Context-Generic Programming.
- The components section leads with "modularity".
- The front matter has no `description`.
- The acknowledgement sits under a second `#` heading.

The sponsorship section and the acknowledgement's text stay as they are.

## How it relates to the knowledge base

The page is the top rung of the conversion ladder in
[formats.md](../../communication-strategy/formats.md), the ask made of the enthusiast profile in
[readers.md](../../communication-strategy/readers.md). Its "consumers need not care about
modularity" argument is the one the [hypershell post](../blog/hypershell-release.md) makes at length,
and [message.md](../../communication-strategy/message.md#when-not-to-reach-for-cgp) could draw on it
more.

## Maintaining it

- The sponsorship section is in the author's first-person voice and candid about finances. Do not
  rewrite it into corporate prose; it is the one place on the site where a single voice belongs.
- The acknowledgement's Hermes SDK link is a legacy resource; raise it with the author rather than
  editing it.
