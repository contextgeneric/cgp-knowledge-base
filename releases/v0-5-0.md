# CGP v0.5.0

The stabilization release: a large batch of additions paired with the most decisive removal in CGP's
history, the deletion of the `Async` trait. It also brings `#[derive(CgpData)]`,
`#[cgp_auto_dispatch]`, monadic computation, and the ability to recover a real `&'static str` from a
type-level string.

- **Tag** — [`v0.5.0`](https://github.com/contextgeneric/cgp/releases/tag/v0.5.0), 12 October 2025
- **Announcement** — [CGP v0.5.0 Release: Auto dispatchers, extensible datatype improvements, monadic computation, RTN emulation, modular serde, and more](https://contextgeneric.dev/blog/v0-5-0-release),
  documented at [website/blog/v0-5-0-release.md](../website/blog/v0-5-0-release.md)
- **Changelog** — [v0.5.0](https://github.com/contextgeneric/cgp/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), the longest
  entry in the file
- **Relation to today** — almost entirely current. This is the oldest release whose *semantics* a
  reader can trust throughout, though not its provider syntax.

## What it introduced

**`#[derive(CgpData)]`** becomes the umbrella derive for extensible data, replacing the need to list
`HasField`, `HasFields`, `BuildField`, `FromVariant`, and `ExtractField` separately, with
`CgpRecord` and `CgpVariant` as the struct- and enum-specific faces.

**`#[cgp_auto_dispatch]`** lifts a plain per-type trait onto an enum whose variants all implement it,
so `HasArea` implemented for `Circle` and `Rectangle` gives `Shape` an implementation for free — and
does so across crate boundaries, with neither the trait nor the enum knowing about the other. It was
introduced as `#[cgp_dispatch]` and renamed within the same release.

**The extensible-data machinery grows in three directions.** `UpdateField` generalizes field mutation
on a partial record, with `BuildField` becoming a blanket impl over it. `finalize_with_default` fills
uninitialized fields from `Default` by applying a natural transformation across the field markers. And
the new `IsOptional` marker gives a builder whose *type* does not change as fields are set, so a
partial record can be filled from dynamic input — the form
[cgp-serde](../projects/cgp-serde/README.md) uses for deserialization. The visitor dispatchers grow
from three to six with mutable and tuple-input variants.

**Monadic computation** lands as the `cgp-monad` crate. Its treatment is *retroactive*: rather than
requiring a higher-kinded type, a monad provider matches on an output type and yields its inner value
type, so `Result` and `Option` become monads without implementing anything on them. `PipeMonadic`
composes handlers that short-circuit, which is what the visitor dispatchers are built from.
`AsyncComputer` arrives alongside as the async counterpart of `Computer`, making the family symmetric
and leaving `Handler` as the async counterpart of `TryComputer`.

**`StaticString`** lets a `Symbol!` produce a genuine `&'static str` during const evaluation. This
required changing the symbol representation to carry its length as a prefix, since counting characters
in a type is not possible without full const generics.

Smaller additions: lifetimes are allowed in CGP trait parameters and are lifted into the new `Life`
type inside `IsProviderFor`, which is what makes a component like
`CanDeserializeValue<'de, Value>` expressible; `#[cgp_getter]` methods may take a non-`self` first
argument; `#[cgp_type]` and `#[cgp_getter]` accept a bare provider name; and `DelegateComponent`'s
`Name` parameter gains a `?Sized` bound.

## What it changed or removed

**`Async` is removed, and with it `HasAsyncErrorType`, `CanRaiseAsyncError`, `CanWrapAsyncError`,
`MaybeSend`, `MaybeSync`, and `MaybeStatic`.** The announcement's explanation is the best account the
project has published of the problem: `Async` existed so that futures returned by generic async
methods would satisfy `tokio::spawn`'s `Send` bound, which meant `Send + Sync` propagating through
code with no other reason to carry it. Return Type Notation would fix this properly but is not close
to stabilizing, so v0.5.0 adopted a **proxy trait** instead — `CanSendRun` beside `CanRun`, whose
method promises a `Send` future and is implemented by hand on the concrete context, where the trait
solver can see through to real types. Providers stay free of `Send` bounds and the guarantee is
recovered exactly where it is needed.

Four renames and one deletion round it out. `symbol!` becomes **`Symbol!`** and desugars differently,
gaining the length prefix; `ι` becomes `ζ` to avoid `rustc`'s `confusable_idents` warning; `Char`
becomes **`Chars`**; generated partial data types gain a **`__Partial`** prefix to keep them out of
the user's namespace; and **`HasInner`** and the `cgp-inner` crate are removed, superseded by
`UseField`. `cgp-field`'s exports are reorganized into submodules, and `CanRun` gains a `Code`
parameter.

One item in the changelog never shipped: `MatchStr`, credited to this release, was deleted before the
tag was cut when `StaticString` superseded it.

## How it relates to v0.8.0

**Current.** [`#[derive(CgpData)]`](../cgp/reference/derives/derive_cgp_data.md),
[`#[cgp_auto_dispatch]`](../cgp/reference/macros/cgp_auto_dispatch.md), the
[optional-field builder](../cgp/reference/traits/optional_fields.md),
[`MapType`](../cgp/reference/traits/map_type.md) with its three markers, the
[dispatch combinators](../cgp/reference/providers/dispatch_combinators.md) in all six forms,
[monadic handlers](../cgp/concepts/monadic-handlers.md) with the
[monad traits](../cgp/reference/traits/monad.md) and
[providers](../cgp/reference/providers/monad_providers.md),
[`StaticFormat` / `StaticString`](../cgp/reference/traits/static_format.md),
[`Life`](../cgp/reference/types/life.md), and
[`Symbol!`](../cgp/reference/macros/symbol.md) over [`Chars`](../cgp/reference/types/chars.md) are all
documented as current.

**The `Send` decision has held.** [Recovering `Send` bounds](../cgp/concepts/send-bounds.md) is still
how CGP handles this, and [`CanRun` / `CanSendRun`](../cgp/reference/components/runner.md) are still
the components. The announcement calls the pattern temporary, pending Return Type Notation; three
releases later it is unchanged, so present it as CGP's current answer rather than as a stopgap.

**Superseded in presentation, not in substance.** Every provider in the announcement is written
inside-out with `#[cgp_provider]`/`#[cgp_new_provider]`;
[`#[cgp_impl]`](../cgp/reference/macros/cgp_impl.md) replaced that two weeks later in
[v0.6.0](v0-6-0.md). `#[cgp_context]` appears throughout and was removed in [v0.7.0](v0-7-0.md). The
`UseDelegate` and `UseInputDelegate` tables it wires are the legacy dispatch form, superseded by the
`open` statement in [v0.8.0](v0-8-0.md). And the `check_components!` syntax changed in
[v0.7.0](v0-7-0.md).

**The migration advice still works but is dated.** The announcement suggests copying `Async` and
`HasAsyncErrorType` into a local crate for a codebase not ready to move. That remains a valid escape
hatch for a project pinned to v0.4.x, but it should not be read as guidance for new code.
