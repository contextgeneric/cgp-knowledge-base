# CGP v0.1.0

The first publication of CGP to crates.io, three and a half months before the paradigm was announced
publicly. Everything structural about CGP is already here — the consumer/provider split, the
type-level wiring table, tag-keyed field access, the abstract error type — under names that have
mostly since changed.

- **Tag** — [`v0.1.0`](https://github.com/contextgeneric/cgp/releases/tag/v0.1.0), 2 September 2024
- **Announcement** — none. The project was not yet public; the
  [launch post](../website/blog/early-preview-announcement.md) came on 19 December 2024, after
  [v0.2.0](v0-2-0.md).
- **Changelog** — none. The repository's
  [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/contextgeneric/cgp/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) begins at v0.2.0.
- **Relation to today** — foundational. The architecture survives; almost none of the spelling does.

## What it introduced

The release publishes fifteen crates, and the split among them is already the one CGP keeps: `cgp` as
the umbrella, `cgp-core` and `cgp-extra` as the two tiers of re-export, `cgp-component` and
`cgp-field` for the two halves of the machinery, `cgp-error` for the abstract error type, and
`cgp-async`, `cgp-sync`, `cgp-run`, and `cgp-inner` around them — each proc-macro crate already
separated from the library crate it generates code for, and each of those split again into a thin
`-macro` shim over a `-macro-lib` implementation, the arrangement that survives today as
`cgp-macro` over `cgp-macro-core`.

The user-facing surface is three component macros and two field macros. **`#[derive_component]`**
turns a trait into a component, taking its arguments positionally in the form
`#[derive_component(NameGetterComponent, NameGetter<MyContext>)]`. **`delegate_components!`** builds
the per-context wiring table, and **`define_components!`** builds a reusable group of wirings.
**`#[derive(HasField)]`** generates tag-keyed accessors, and **`symbol!`** — lowercase — builds the
type-level string that keys them.

What matters is that the *shape* of CGP is settled at this point. A trait becomes a pair, an
implementation targets a provider struct the crate owns, and a concrete context chooses among
implementations by populating a type-level table. Everything the base documents as
[bypassing coherence](../cgp/concepts/coherence.md) and
[consumer and provider traits](../cgp/concepts/consumer-and-provider-traits.md) is already true here.

## What it changed or removed

Nothing — this is the first release.

## How it relates to v0.8.0

Every name in this release has moved, but every idea in it has stayed.

- **`#[derive_component]` became [`#[cgp_component]`](../cgp/reference/macros/cgp_component.md)** in
  [v0.2.0](v0-2-0.md), which also replaced the positional arguments with named ones; the short
  positional form returned in [v0.4.0](v0-4-0.md) in a different shape.
- **`define_components!` became `cgp_preset!`** in [v0.2.0](v0-2-0.md), and was removed entirely in
  [v0.8.0](v0-8-0.md) when [namespaces](../cgp/concepts/namespaces.md) replaced the preset mechanism.
- **`delegate_components!` survives unchanged in name** and is
  [still the wiring macro](../cgp/reference/macros/delegate_components.md), though it has grown array
  keys, generic lists, the `new` aggregate form, the `open` statement, and namespace statements since.
- **`#[derive(HasField)]` survives** as
  [the same derive](../cgp/reference/derives/derive_has_field.md), but its associated type was renamed
  from `Field` to `Value` in [v0.2.0](v0-2-0.md).
- **`symbol!` became [`Symbol!`](../cgp/reference/macros/symbol.md)** in [v0.5.0](v0-5-0.md), when the
  capital signalled that it expands to a type, and its expansion gained a length prefix so a
  `&'static str` could be recovered from it.
- **`cgp-inner` and its `HasInner` trait were removed** in [v0.5.0](v0-5-0.md), superseded by
  [`UseField`](../cgp/reference/providers/use_field.md), which composes contexts more flexibly.
- **`cgp-async` and `cgp-sync` are gone** in their v0.1.0 form. The `Async` alias they existed for was
  removed in [v0.5.0](v0-5-0.md) in favour of the
  [`Send`-recovery pattern](../cgp/concepts/send-bounds.md).

The practical consequence for an agent: code pinned to v0.1.0 is unrecognizable at the surface but
translates mechanically, because no concept it uses has been dropped — only renamed or subsumed.
